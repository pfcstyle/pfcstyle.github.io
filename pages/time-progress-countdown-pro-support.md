## App summary

Time Progress Countdown Pro is a lightweight buy-once iOS app for:

- countdown and count-up events
- Year Progress
- widgets
- full-screen focus mode
- optional local reminders

The app does not require an account and does not rely on a server.

## What to include in a support request

To help reproduce an issue, include:

- device model
- iOS version
- app version (`1.0 (1)`)
- what you expected to happen
- what actually happened
- screenshots if relevant

## Common topics

### Notifications / reminders

Reminders are local notifications created by the user for individual events.
If notifications are disabled, reminder delivery will not work until they are re-enabled in iOS Settings.

### Widgets

Widgets show local countdown and Year Progress information from the app's on-device data.
If a widget looks stale, reopen the app and verify the underlying event data is still present.

### Data model

The app stores its event data locally on the device.
Deleting the app may remove locally stored data.
